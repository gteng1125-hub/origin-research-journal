How it works

Five steps from submission to publication.

  1. Submit by email

    Send your manuscript to the address below. There is no account to create and no platform to learn.

  2. Editorial screening

    We check that the paper is in scope, complete and original. Submissions outside our scope are returned quickly so you can place them elsewhere.

  3. Peer review

    Your paper is read by reviewers with background in the subject. They assess the research question, whether the method answers it, whether the evidence supports the claims, and whether the writing is clear.

  4. Decision

    Accept, revise and resubmit, or decline — with written comments in every case. Most authors hear back within about two weeks.

  5. Revision and publication

    If revisions are requested you respond to the comments point by point. On acceptance the $100 publication fee applies, and the paper is copy-edited and published.

What reviewers look for

A question stated clearly enough to be answered; a method suited to that question; evidence a reader can check; and limitations the author names rather than hides. A negative or inconclusive result is publishable if the work behind it is sound.

What we do not publish

Work without a research question; manuscripts under review at another journal; writing generated by an AI system and presented as the author's own; and work that cannot be described in enough detail for a reader to evaluate it.

Editorial policies

The rules we hold submissions to, and hold ourselves to.

Peer review

Every paper that passes editorial screening is read by reviewers with subject background. Reviewers do not see papers they have a personal or mentoring connection to. Authors receive the substance of the reviews with the decision.

Authorship

The student who carried out the work is the author. Teachers, mentors and supervisors are named in an acknowledgements section unless they contributed enough to the research itself to warrant co-authorship. Everyone listed as an author must have agreed to the submission.

Originality

Submissions must be the author's own work, not previously published, and not under review elsewhere. Sources must be cited. Manuscripts are screened for copied text, and suspected plagiarism ends consideration.

Use of AI tools

AI tools may be used for language editing and must be disclosed in the submission. AI systems cannot be listed as authors. Text, data or analysis generated by an AI system and presented as the author's own is grounds for rejection, and for retraction if discovered after publication.

Data and materials

Authors should be able to supply the data, code or materials behind their results on reasonable request. Human-subjects research must describe how consent was obtained.

Corrections and retractions

Errors found after publication are corrected in a dated note attached to the paper. Work found to be fabricated, plagiarised or seriously flawed is retracted, with the reason stated.

Withdrawal

You may withdraw a submission at any point before publication at no cost.

Fees

One fee: $100, on acceptance. Submission and review are free, and there is no charge to withdraw or to resubmit after revision. We do not offer paid fast-track review, and paying does not influence a decision — the fee is charged only after a paper has already been accepted on its merits.

Submit your paper

By email. No account, no portal.

Send your submission to

originresearchjournal@gmail.com

Use the subject line Submission — [your paper's title]

Attach the manuscript as a PDF or Word file, and include in the body of the email:

  • An abstract of 150–250 words
  • Your name, school, year group and email address
  • The name of a mentor or supervisor, if you had one, and what they contributed
  • A sentence confirming the work is original and not under review elsewhere
  • A sentence describing any use of AI tools, or stating that none were used

We acknowledge every submission. If you have not heard from us within three days, the email did not arrive — please send it again.
